# Candidate epitaphs

Alternate lines considered for this entry. The chosen line won because
it lands as a complete little story in one breath: a fond cliché
("doing what he loved") undercut by a very specific, very human cause
of death, with no setup the reader has to decode.

1. "I said 'watch this' one too many times."
   — Punchy and true to life, but the joke is a familiar meme shape;
     the chosen line's twist feels fresher.
2. "Well, this escalated quickly."
   — Quotable, but it reads like a caption, not a dying man's own
     words carved in stone.
3. "He finally found the snooze button."
   — Gentle and sweet, but more cute than funny; the laugh is too soft
     for a head-to-head judge.
4. "Turns out the expiration date applied to me too."
   — A nice double meaning, but it needs a beat to parse; the chosen
     line lands on first read.
5. "Do not resuscitate. I have seen the group chat."
   — Strong surprise, but the second sentence leans on a modern
     reference that dates the stone.
